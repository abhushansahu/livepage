A selection on a live page is a decision site

Most tools treat the web as something to clip and file. That misses the moment that actually matters: you are in the habitat of the page, mid-thought, and you need a cheap way to couple, mark fog, or park a why.

Why the live page

Reader views strip the visual rhetoric that steered your attention. Sidebars, diagrams, and the spatial memory of “it was halfway down” are part of how trails form. Annotation that only works after capture is annotation of a specimen, not of the living animal.

Comments as conversations

A margin note is not a dead sticker. It is an unfinished cognitive thread. It can fork when you and an agent take different readings of the same span. The agent should receive a parsed packet of unique blocks, and should answer strictly the ask — not a generic summary of the whole internet.

Compost, not a second brain

Obsidian is where cooled thought becomes durable. The dashboard is mission control for open loops: waiting pages, bookmarks, parsed blocks. Infinite feeds are refused until you snapshot a stable view, because a highlight needs an anchor that can be found again.
